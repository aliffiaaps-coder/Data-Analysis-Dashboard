## Project Objective
A retail company has recently developed a transaction recording application. The Product Manager (PM) has requested assistance from a Data Analyst to analyze sales performance.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
1. Demonstrate overall transaction performance
2. Identifying sales trends
3. Evaluating the performance of each payment method
4. Identifying best-selling products
5. Tracking discount-related spending
6. Summarizing transactions by product and customer

## Project Insight
1. Sales peak season around May 2022 and August 2022.
2. A steep drop occurred between October 2022 and December 2022.
3. Transactions by payment method were dominated by COD (67.6%), followed by Payaxis (17.2%).
4. Transactions by category are highest in Others (26.8%), Beauty & Grooming (20.2%), and Mobiles & Tablets (17.6%).
5. The total discount budget spent is $33.83m.
6. Based on the Product Summary, the IDROID_BALRX7-Gold and Macbook Pro generated massive profits without requiring discounts.

## Business Recommendations
1. Implement an expansion strategy to increase new customer acquisition.
2. Conduct a thorough audit to identify the causes of the sharp decline in Q4 2022.
3. Minimize cancellation rates and COD delivery failures as much as possible.
4. Secure stock supplies for the Mobiles & Tablets category and top-performing products to prevent stockouts.
5. Utilize the discount budget $33.83m to drive sales of slow-moving products and to acquire new users.
6. Offer purchase contracts or special credit to B2B customers to ensure stable business cash flow in the coming years.
